# Current offer analysis

**Author**: CatrinaB \
**Version**: 1 \
**Publication date**: 4 August 2026


## 1. Purpose
This document aims to explore the top ten period tracking apps available in Google Play store. In this case, "top ten" refers to the first non-sponsored ten results returned when searching "period tracker" on Google Play Store[^1]. In addition to these ten, an eleventh app will be analised 
due to its "privacy-focused" marketing and good review given by the Mozilla Foundation.

[^1]: The top ten results depend on each user's Google Play Store behaviour and download history.

## 2. Scope
This analysis focuses on features and issues related to the main use of period tracking mobile applications, i.e. symptom and mood logging throughtout the menstrual cycle. As such, aspect like ease of use, ad intrusiveness, graphical user interphace are in scope, 
while knowledge databases or forum functionalities fall outside of it. However, insights regarding the current day of the cycle may fall in scope if they refer to hormones and/or symptoms.

## 3. Current offer
### 1. [Period Calendar Period Tracker by Simple Design Ltd.](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.popularapp.periodcalendar)
Rating (26 July 2026): 4.8/5, 4.88 million reviews

#### Developer
The developer's [website](https://simpledesign.ltd/) does not mention where they're based , but according to [AndroidRank](https://www.androidrank.org/developer?id=Simple+Design+Ltd.) the company operates out of Hong Kong.

#### Data policy

- no sharing with third parties
- collects information about health, device ID, app interactions, installed apps, app performance
- collects name and e-mail address
- data is encrypted in transit
- allows requests for data deletion

[Mozilla Foundation privacy review](https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/nothing-personal/period-calendar-privacy-review/)

#### Issues

- ads (asks for consent first thing when it opens, displays a full-page ad before going to the home screen)
- lags on home screen when scrolling down
- gives analysis on non-existent data
- design is either pastel, black (one option) or old
- some pets are locked behind a paywall
- some icons are childish (condom, female orgasm)
- overwhelming symptom logging screen
- all symptoms have a 1-4 scale (even if it makes no sense for some of the symptoms)
- cannot log contraceptive unless permission is given to send notificatins (for reminders, even if reminders are turned off)
- overview/analysis is only available for intercourse and for cycle duration, not symptoms
- takes about 3-5 seconds to open
- no clear indication data is saved when it is selected
- no explanation or information about moods, symptoms

#### Features
- partner mode (needs a pairing code)
- offers the option to restore data from previous use via Google Account, email attachment, dropbox, cloud storage or local storage
- can skip intro questions
- can add previous periods quickly
- goals, physical data, pregnancy mode are available in the settings screen
- can change themes (colours)
- can modify the symptoms that can be logged and their name
- can export data as email to someone
- information about cycle phase, conception chance, cervical discharge and/or cramps relief for "today"
- works without internet
- timeline (agenda view of every day that has had some information logged)
- self care information (i.e. yoga poses, massages) available without internet

### 2. [Flo Period Tracker by Flo Health Inc.](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.iggymedia.periodtracker)
Rating (26 July 2026): 4.7/5, 5.01 million reviews

#### Developer

Flo Health Inc. is based in the UK, with offices in Lithuania, the Netherlands, and the USA ([source](https://flo.health/contact-us)).
The developer was [sued for sharing user data with advertisers](https://www.hipaajournal.com/flo-health-google-flurry-59-5m-settlement-privacy-lawsuit/), despite their reassurances that [they never sold users' data](https://flo.health/flo-privacy-faqs).

#### Data policy

- no sharing with third parties
- collects information about health, device ID, app interactions, in-app search history and messages, photos and videos, financial information, location, app performance
- collects name, e-mail address, and user ID
- data is encrypted in transit
- allows requests for data deletion

[Mozilla Foundation privacy review](https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/nothing-personal/flo-app-privacy-review/)

#### Issues
- cannot skip first round of intro questions about age, purpose of use
- it takes more than 20 different screens with information, questions, and self-advertising before one can begin using the app
- asks the user to "commit" to using Flo by holding down for 5 seconds on a fingerprint-shaped symbol located in the general area of on-screen fingerprint sensors
- displays subscription options and a discounted-price "lottery" before displaying the home screen
- almost all information related to the cycle (including pregnancy chance) is locked behind a paywall
- subscription screen pops up during use
- "assistant" pops up after symptom logging
- cannot modify symptoms displayed
- no information or details about the symptoms
- "anonymous mode" is not advertised until the user wants to delete their account
- colour scheme is pastel pink, no options to change

#### Features
- allows (limited) control of tracked information
- allows connecting to Health Connect, Oura ring
- can add previous periods quickly
- check-in option: asks if the user went on a walk, had any backacke, etc.
- can show, hide, and reorder symptom categories
- has "anonymous mode" that separates health data from personal information

## 4. Summary of issues and features

### Most significant issues[^3]
1. An active Internet connection is required for the app to work.
2. Symptom list cannot be modified and/or symptoms cannot be customised.
3. The default colour theme and the icons used are childish and very pink.
4. Invasive and/or persistent advertisements for the premium functionalities.
5. Insights into the menstrual cycle and/or connections betwen it and the user's symptoms are pay-walled.
6. Data gets shared with unknown third party companies, even if the developer declares it is not.

### Most important features[^3]
1. Data is not shared with third party companies; ideally, the data does not leave the device.
2. Most or all features don't require Internet or an account to be used.
3. The list of symptoms and/or the symptoms themselves can be modified (the order, whether they're shown or hidden, the name, intensity levels...).
4. Health report can be easily exported.
5. App default colour scheme (and icons) is not pink and/or childish; ideally theme can be changed.
6. The tutorial and/or introductory questions can be skipped and revisited at a later date.
7. There is a timeline (agenda) view available.
8. Some information regarding the hormonal levels and their influence on energy, mood, or symptoms is available. Ideally, corelations are also made between symptoms and the phase of the menstrual cycle.

[^3]: Based on the author's personal preferences.
